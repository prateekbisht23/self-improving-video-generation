## Why this candidate

**Hypothesis:** If we enable snap_cuts to sentence and turn on captions_follow_reference, then narrative structure improves because cuts align with complete sentences instead of splitting mid-thought, and caption matching succeeds by respecting reference style.

**Targets:** narrative.structure, style.caption_match

**Policy changes:** `{'snap_cuts': 'sentence', 'captions_follow_reference': True}`

**Planner prompt addition:**

```
- Align all clip in and out points to sentence boundaries using word timings to prevent mid-sentence cuts.
```

**Expected effect:** Significantly improves narrative.structure by eliminating abrupt cuts and incomplete sentences, and resolves style.caption_match failures by disabling captions when the reference lacks them.

**Risks:** Sentence snapping might slightly shift total target durations, which could require minor adjustments from fixup.
